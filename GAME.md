## Attributes — Body, Mind & Soul

The player manages three attributes representing their physical, psychological, and spiritual condition. Each attribute has two dangerous extremes and a stable middle range. Choices shift these balances, potentially triggering supernatural events, altered behavior, or distinct endings.

| Attribute | Low | Balanced | High |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Body** | Exhaustion | Vitality | Frenzy |
| **Mind** | Dissociation | Lucidity | Delirium |
| **Soul** | Spiritual Blindness | Attunement | Mediumship |

### Body — Physical Control
- **Exhaustion:** The body refuses to act. Weakness, sluggishness, and eventual collapse.
- **Vitality:** Balanced energy, endurance, and voluntary control.
- **Frenzy:** The body refuses to stop. Restlessness, involuntary movements, and uncontrollable impulses.

### Mind — Perception and Reason
- **Dissociation:** Detachment from reality, emotional numbness, and fading awareness.
- **Lucidity:** Coherent perception, reasoning, and judgment.
- **Delirium:** Overwhelming perceptions, hallucinations, and inability to distinguish reality from imagination.

### Soul — Supernatural Sensitivity
- **Spiritual Blindness:** Cannot perceive supernatural forces, becoming vulnerable to their influence without realizing it.
- **Attunement:** Recognizes spiritual presences and influences while maintaining protective boundaries.
- **Mediumship:** Excessive supernatural sensitivity attracts entities and weakens spiritual boundaries, risking involuntary channeling and possession.

### Gameplay Rules
- Each attribute operates on a bipolar scale, with a relatively safe middle range.
- Card decisions increase or decrease one or more attributes.
- Approaching an extreme introduces increasingly severe narrative and gameplay consequences.
- Reaching an extreme triggers a critical event, transformation, or ending.
- Both extremes are dangerous for different reasons; increasing an attribute is not inherently beneficial.
- Attributes may interact, allowing instability in one dimension to affect the others.
